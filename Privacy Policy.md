Privacy Policy for Rift Security

Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how RIFT, a Discord bot developed by Tidez, collects, uses, and shares your personal information. By using RIFT, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect your Discord user ID, username, server IDs, and any data you voluntarily provide through interactions with the bot.

We do not collect emails, phone numbers, or other sensitive personal data unless explicitly provided.

Some data, such as messages or prompts used by the bot for its functions, may be processed by third-party services (e.g., Discord) to generate responses.

How We Use Your Information

To operate and improve Rift, including responding to your commands and providing support.

For analytics, development of new features, and compliance with legal requirements.

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties except where required by law or for services directly related to the bot’s function.

Data Retention and Security

Your data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the bot's services or as required by law.

We take reasonable security measures to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You can stop using the bot at any time.

You may request deletion of any personal data the bot has stored about you.

If you have concerns about data usage, contact g2.tidez.
